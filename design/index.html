Kitsu TV · Kitsu at ten feet

Storyboard

Thirteen screens, in the order a person meets them, each drawn at 1920×1080 and scaled to fit. Open one to drive it: arrows are the D-pad, Enter is OK, Backspace is BACK, the number keys switch a screen's states. The UI Kit is the source of truth for every token and component here.

01Find the servertv-connect.html
Open

mDNS finds the server; an older API is shown, not hidden; the manual address is the fallback for an isolating Wi-Fi. States: found · none · manual.

02Pairingtv-pairing.html
Open

The code is the screen; it is typed on the server, never on the remote. The television polls and continues on its own. States: waiting · admitted · expired.

03Who is watchingtv-profiles.html
Open

Profiles as big faces; a closed one asks for a four-digit PIN on a pad, in a dialog that traps the cursor.

04Hometv-home.html
Open

The hero is what was being watched, with one button. Shelves below: continue, latest arrivals, genres, collections. Rows remember where you left them.

05Filmstv-films.html
Open

A rail of genres and filters on the left, a wall of posters on the right, sort chips above. LEFT from the first column reaches the rail.

06Collectionstv-collections.html
Open

A wall of nodes, not of posters — a collection has no face of its own, and borrowing the first film’s is a lie that looks convincing. States: arranged · empty.

07Genrestv-genres.html
Open

The same wall over the genres. The name and the count are what the choice is made on; nothing here pretends to be artwork.

08Inside a nodetv-node.html
Open

What is filed under one genre or one collection — one page for both, the eyebrow the only difference. No rail and no sort: this page IS the narrowing, and a collection’s order is the thing that made it one.

09Kepttv-favourites.html
Open

The household’s own shelf. The heading names what they DID, which is what lets the empty state name the gesture that fills it. States: kept · empty.

10Titletv-title.html
Open

Artwork owns the top; name, one line, the facts that matter, and the playback decision. Below: cast, more like this, and what is on the disk.

11Seriestv-series.html
Open

Seasons as chips, episodes as a list that reads downwards: still, number, name, what is left. An episode not on the disk is shown, disabled, and says why.

12Playertv-player.html
Open

Two thin bands of chrome over the picture; direct play or transcode always in the corner; the clock time it ends. States: paused · playing · ending (up next).

13Searchtv-search.html
Open

A letter grid where the thumb rests, results as letters land, and every result says why it matched. States: results · empty.
